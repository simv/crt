## Summary
Stop CRT from writing into a project without saying so. `crt` no longer initialises a project on its own; `crt init` prints its plan, asks once on a terminal, writes `.crt/README.md`, `.crt/tasks/`, `.crt/config.json`, the two `.gitignore` lines and a marked CRT section into `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md` that tells the project's agents what `.crt/` is — each announced — and ends with the one-line snippet for the detected framework. A new `/crt:init` skill applies that snippet to the app; `/crt:serve` runs embedded and offers `/crt:init` when the project is not set up; `crt doctor` gains `mode`, `integration` and `instructions` rows. `docs/PRD-embedded.md` §5.5, §6.4, §6.5 (F-99…F-106, N-19) and their F-110 rows.
